04 — Key Findings

Six things the data actually says.

Fear Paradox
Fear beats Greed on total profit
Despite lower win rates than Greed, Fear produces $3.36M — the most of any regime. Traders take larger, more decisive positions when sentiment is negative. Avg trade size in Fear ($7,816) is 2.5× Extreme Greed's $3,112.
Extreme Fear Trap
"Buy the blood" doesn't hold up
Extreme Fear is statistically the worst regime: lowest win rate (76.2%), lowest avg PnL ($71), and short positions collapse to a 63.5% win rate. Execution risk during panic is severely underestimated.
Volume Paradox
Markets are loudest when scared
Fear generates $483M in volume — 4× Extreme Greed's $124.5M. Liquidations, stop cascades, and emotional trading fuel this. Market makers and systematic traders benefit most from this liquidity.
Long Bias
Longs dominate across all regimes
Long positions maintain 86–90% win rates across every sentiment state, reflecting the structural upward bias of the 2023–2025 crypto bull cycle. Shorts are only competitive in Extreme Greed (89.4%).
Dec 2024 Outlier
One month = 29% of all profits
$3.0M in December 2024 is attributable to HYPE token's launch during a Greed regime. Excluding this event, the monthly average is ~$350K — far more representative of sustainable performance.
Strategy Divergence
Selectivity beats frequency
Top traders split: high-frequency (Wallet #1: 6,279 trades, $341/trade) vs high-conviction (Wallet #2: 1,732 trades, $924/trade). The high-conviction approach yields 2.7× the per-trade return.
